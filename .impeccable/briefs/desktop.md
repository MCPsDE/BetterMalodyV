## Scope and modes

- **Manager — Read:** Malody V players read four one-sentence entries in one selectable, scrolling document from editable local Markdown, with embedded fallback. The fourth describes manually reenacting the owner's local replay through normal play, scoring and result submission. Refresh/F5 and update checking are supporting commands; the fixed footer carries appearance and sponsorship.
- **Installer — Operate:** Players select or correct the game directory, inspect BepInEx compatibility, then install/repair, open the manager or uninstall. Path diagnosis, environment feedback and a scrollable log support the task. The primary install/repair action, progress and concise operation status stay outside the content viewport. Invalid, installed, busy, completed and failed states govern available commands.
- **Game extension — Operate:** Three equal status toggles use quiet translucent fills and an actual checkmark to show selection. The native room song area gains a measured Update action for a host’s outdated chart, while members automatically replace a verified host share. Search text and song title/artist space remain separate from actions. Cached summaries keep whole-library scanning out of filtering. Startup and periodic updates use a hidden helper and the game's native dialog for progress, verification, ready countdown and failure. Play, editing and unsafe room states defer the notice and normal exit/restart; the 3-second countdown begins only after the ready notice is visible. Local score details add “重演并上传” / “Replay & upload” beside Cancel/Watch, with native type, a neutral translucent fill and retained trash/share controls. The same-UID logged-in .mrv requires an exact chart hash and supported original settings; only recorded inputs enter normal play, judgement and result submission. Evidence is isolated native scenes and synthetic data; no live submission or server acceptance is established.
